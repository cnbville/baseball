What the numbers mean

Baseball basics

SP / RP
Starting pitcher (starts the game and throws the first five innings or more, about once every five games) and relief pitcher (comes on later for an inning or two, like an impact sub). Each is only compared with his own kind.
HS, JuCo, College
High school, junior college (a two-year college) and four-year college. "College SP" means college starting pitchers. Players are only compared within their own level.
Positions
C catcher, 1B / 2B / 3B first, second and third base, SS shortstop, LF / CF / RF left, centre and right field, DH designated hitter (bats but doesn't field).
Innings and plate appearances
Playing time. A full game is 9 innings; a hitter gets about 4 plate appearances a game. "86⅓ innings" means 86 innings plus one out.
ERA
Earned runs allowed per 9 innings, the classic pitching number. It depends on the fielders behind him and on luck, which is why FIP and SIERA are used for the score.
BABIP
Batting average on balls in play: how often a ball put in play becomes a hit. Over a short season it swings a lot on luck, so it's used as a luck check.
